How catering works

Everything you need to know before you call

Ordering

By noon the business day before

Call (925) 930-6323, email cateringorders@sunrisebistrocatering.com, or send the quote form below. In November and December, earlier is better. There is no online checkout for catering; a person confirms every order.

Delivery & pickup

Seven days a week

Delivery daily 7 AM–4 PM across Walnut Creek and the East Bay; pickup daily 7 AM–3 PM at 1559 Botelho Drive. Ask about delivery fees when you order.

Minimums

Set by the menu, not by us

Box lunches and platters have no minimum. Buffets serve 15 or more; the Informal and Light Refreshment buffets serve 25 or more; the Lox & Bagel platter is priced per person for 20 or more. Every buffet can be packaged individually for $3 per person.

Office hours

Mon–Fri 9 AM–5 PM

Weekend and evening requests are answered the next business morning. Send the form any time and we will call you back.

Full service

Staffed events, booked ahead

Weddings, receptions and memorials with servers and setup are booked about three months in advance. We do our best to accommodate every request, memorial receptions above all.

Dietary

GF, vegan and kosher-style

Gluten-free and vegan boxes on request (with fresh fruit and chia-seed pudding); Mariposa gluten-free bread +$2.50. Kosher-style menus are prepared in our kitchen, which is not a kosher kitchen.

Questions

Catering FAQ

How far ahead do I need to order?

By 12:00 PM the business day before your event. Larger events and the holiday season need more notice, and staffed full-service events are booked about three months out.

Do you deliver on weekends?

Yes. Delivery runs seven days a week, 7 AM–4 PM. The catering office itself is open Monday to Friday, 9 AM–5 PM, so weekend orders need to be placed by noon on Friday.

Is there an order minimum?

Minimums are set per menu. Box lunches and platters have no minimum. Most buffets serve 15 or more; the Informal and Light Refreshment buffets serve 25 or more; the Lox & Bagel platter is 20 people or more.

Can I order catering online?

Not yet. Every catering order is confirmed by a person, by phone or email. Bistro pickup and delivery orders can be placed online through Toast.

Do you handle gluten-free, vegan and kosher-style requests?

Yes. Gluten-free and vegan boxes are available on request, gluten-free bread is $2.50 extra, and we offer kosher-style buffets. Our kitchen is not a kosher kitchen.

What is included with a buffet?

Paper and plastic goods are included with every buffet, and every buffet can be packaged individually for $3 per person. Rolls and butter come with the comfort-food pans and the Upscale buffet. Ask about chafing dishes, setup and staffing when you order.
