Privacy Policy

Draft for review by the owners and counsel before it goes live. Effective date: to be set on publication.

Who we are

Sunrise Bistro & Catering (Joroda, Inc.), 1559 Botelho Drive, Walnut Creek, CA 94596, operates this website. Questions about this policy can be sent to cateringorders@sunrisebistrocatering.com or (925) 930-6323.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you send a catering quote request or sign up for our newsletter, we collect the details you enter: your name, company, phone number, email address, event date and details, delivery address and any notes.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, this site uses cookies and similar tools to measure how it is used. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read, and may use advertising tools from Meta to measure the results of our ads. These tools may collect your device type, browser, approximate location, pages viewed and the site that referred you.

Online ordering and gift cards are handled by Toast on its own website. Toast's privacy policy applies to orders, payments and gift cards.

How we use it

To answer your catering request and prepare your quote; to fulfil orders and gift cards; to send the newsletter you signed up for; to understand how the website is used and improve it; and to measure our advertising.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who work on our behalf: our website host, our email and newsletter provider, Google (analytics), Meta (advertising measurement) and Toast (ordering and gift cards). Each may use the information only to provide its service to us, and we may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter using the link in any email. You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to cateringorders@sunrisebistrocatering.com; we will respond within 30 days. You can block cookies in your browser settings, and Google offers a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
