Our kitchen

Forty-five years of doing it from scratch

Sunrise opened in downtown Walnut Creek in 1981 and is still family-owned and operated. The reason regulars keep their table is simple: almost everything on the plate was made in this kitchen the same morning.

  • Breads, pastries, quiches, croissants and morning buns baked fresh daily in house
  • Dressings, sauces, berry compote, whipped cream and mascarpone, all house-made
  • Brisket smoked in house, corned beef chopped for the hash, Mary's chicken on the grill
  • Gluten-free bread and a $2 gluten-free option on the griddle; vegan burritos, hashes and bowls
